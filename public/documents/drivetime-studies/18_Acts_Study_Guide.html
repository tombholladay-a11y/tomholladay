ABOUT DRIVETIME DEVOTIONS
SMALL GROUP STUDIES

Welcome to this special Drivetime Devotions, small group study. This 6-week study is designed so that you can enjoy Drivetime Devotions as a podcast throughout the week and then talk about it with your small group. This will allow for more time in your small group for discussion, application, and fellowship.

The purpose of Drivetime Devotions is to help you know God's Word and put it into practice. It's usually better to study small sections of God's Word each day instead of a larger section once a week. Studying the Bible is like eating—you'll be healthier if you eat small amounts each day rather than one big meal a week! Episodes are designed to be listened to once per day, five days a week. In the event you miss a day or two, you can still stay on schedule.

A FEW TIPS BEFORE YOU DIG IN

LISTEN

Prepare yourself before each study by listening to each week's five daily podcast episodes (one per day).

CHECKING IN

Open each meeting with a time for everyone to check in on each other's progress with the podcast. Accountability is a key to success in this study!

KEY VERSE

Each week you will find a key verse (or verses) from the chapter you are studying. You may want to memorize this verse together as a group.

6
ACTS: A SMALL GROUP STUDY

DISCOVERY QUESTIONS

These are questions for group discussion. Please don't feel pressured to discuss every single question. The material in this study is meant to be your servant, not your master. Therefore there is no reason to rush through your discussions. Give everyone ample time to share their thoughts.

Some questions will reference the verses from which they are taken. Many groups will want to have someone read those verses accompanying the question. There is something powerful about hearing the Bible read aloud together as a group.

LIVING ON PURPOSE

This section helps you encourage each other in balancing God's five purposes of evangelism, discipleship, fellowship, ministry, and worship. Each week, there will be a prayer, practical action, or personal assignment that focuses on one of these purposes.

PRAYING TOGETHER

At the end of each session you will find suggestions for your group prayer time. Praying together is one of the greatest privileges of small group life. Please don't take it for granted.

Get ready for God to do incredible things in your life as you begin the adventure of learning more deeply about the most exciting message in the world: the truth about God!

WEEK ONE

CHECKING IN

  • What is one interesting fact about your family or where you grew up that we might not know?

KEY VERSE

"But you will receive power when the Holy Spirit comes on you; and you will be my witnesses in Jerusalem, and in all Judea and Samaria, and to the ends of the earth."
Acts 1:8 (NIV)

DISCOVERY QUESTIONS

  1. Looking at the command to wait in verses 4-5, how do you personally handle seasons of waiting, and how can we help each other depend on God rather than rushing ahead? Verses 1:4-5
  2. Based on the call in verse 8, where do you feel God is currently asking you to be a bold witness, and how can we encourage you to overcome the fears holding you back? Verses 1:8
10
ACTS: A SMALL GROUP STUDY
  1. Reflecting on the unity in verses 12-14, how do you maintain "one accord" with believers when stress arises, and how can we support each other during disagreements? Verses 1:12-14
  2. When considering the outpouring in verses 1-4, when have you felt the empowering presence of the Holy Spirit, and how can we seek that power together for our daily lives? Verses 2:1-4
  3. Looking at the early church in verses 42-47, what practical steps can we take together to devote ourselves more deeply to fellowship and prayer this week? Verses 2:42-47
  4. Reflecting on the healing in verses 1-6, how can we grow in noticing the hurts of people around us, and how can we challenge each other to offer genuine spiritual hope instead of just quick fixes? Verses 3:1-6

LIVING ON PURPOSE

WORSHIP

We worship God not just by singing, but by waiting on His timing, depending entirely on the Holy Spirit's power, and boldly proclaiming His wonders to those in need. Let's practice living empowered, Spirit-filled lives that point others to Jesus this week.

PRAYING TOGETHER

Let's pray for patience in our seasons of waiting and for a fresh filling of the Holy Spirit to empower our witness.
