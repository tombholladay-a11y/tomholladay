ABOUT DRIVETIME DEVOTIONS
SMALL GROUP STUDIES

Welcome to this special Drivetime Devotions, small group study. This 5-week study is designed so that you can enjoy Drivetime Devotions as a podcast throughout the week and then talk about it with your small group. This will allow for more time in your small group for discussion, application, and fellowship.

The purpose of Drivetime Devotions is to help you know God's Word and put it into practice. It's usually better to study small sections of God's Word each day instead of a larger section once a week. Studying the Bible is like eating—you'll be healthier if you eat small amounts each day rather than one big meal a week! Episodes are designed to be listened to once per day, five days a week. In the event you miss a day or two, you can still stay on schedule.

A FEW TIPS BEFORE YOU DIG IN

LISTEN

Prepare yourself before each study by listening to each week's five daily podcast episodes (one per day).

CHECKING IN

Open each meeting with a time for everyone to check in on each other's progress with the podcast. Accountability is a key to success in this study!

KEY VERSE

Each week you will find a key verse (or verses) from the chapter you are studying. You may want to memorize this verse together as a group.

6
PSALMS 51-75: A SMALL GROUP STUDY

DISCOVERY QUESTIONS

These are questions for group discussion. Please don't feel pressured to discuss every single question. The material in this study is meant to be your servant, not your master. Therefore there is no reason to rush through your discussions. Give everyone ample time to share their thoughts.

Some questions will reference the verses from which they are taken. Many groups will want to have someone read those verses accompanying the question. There is something powerful about hearing the Bible read aloud together as a group.

LIVING ON PURPOSE

This section helps you encourage each other in balancing God's five purposes of evangelism, discipleship, fellowship, ministry, and worship. Each week, there will be a prayer, practical action, or personal assignment that focuses on one of these purposes.

PRAYING TOGETHER

At the end of each session you will find suggestions for your group prayer time. Praying together is one of the greatest privileges of small group life. Please don't take it for granted.

Get ready for God to do incredible things in your life as you begin the adventure of learning more deeply about the most exciting message in the world: the truth about God!

As you go through each session, the Holy Spirit may bring to mind different people in your life. You may even want to write their names down beside a point, phrase, or verse in the margins. Take the opportunity to share God's Word in a friend's life during the week.

WEEK ONE

CHECKING IN

  • Looking back at our last meeting, what is one truth or application that you were able to practice this past week?
  • When you think about someone who is a truly supportive friend, what is one quality they consistently demonstrate?

KEY VERSE

"Create in me a pure heart, O God, and renew a steadfast spirit within me."
Psalm 51:10

DISCOVERY QUESTIONS

  1. David's confession points us away from making excuses and toward God's washing mercy. When you feel the heavy weight of your own mistakes, how can we encourage one another to turn toward God's unfailing love instead of hiding from Him? Psalm 51:1-2
  2. David asks God for a completely re-created heart rather than just a quick fix. What is a specific area in your life where you need God to create a fresh sense of joy, and how can this group support you in that renewal? Psalm 51:10-12
10
PSALMS 51-75: A SMALL GROUP STUDY
  1. We see the picture of a flourishing olive tree thriving in the house of God rather than trusting in wealth or deceit. How can we as a group help each other stay rooted in God's unfailing love when the temporary success of the world seems so appealing? Psalm 52:8-9
  2. David contrasts the destruction of boasting and deceit with the eternal goodness of hoping in God's name. In what ways do you struggle to let go of self-reliance, and how can we point each other back to trusting God's steadfastness? Psalm 52:1-9
  3. The fool lives as if there is no God, putting hope in circumstances or self instead of the Lord. In what practical areas of your week are you most tempted to live as a 'practical atheist', and how can we remind each other of God's active presence? Psalm 53:1-3
  4. The Psalm contrasts the fear of evildoers with the salvation that God brings to His people. When anxiety about the future creeps in, how can we help each other seek God's kingdom first rather than relying on our own plans? Psalm 53:4-6
  5. David faced betrayal from his own countrymen yet chose to rely on God's name as his ultimate defense. When you experience hurt or betrayal from someone close to you, how can we pray for one another to look to God rather than seeking revenge? Psalm 54:1-5
  6. Recognizing God as his helper, David commits to praising God even before his circumstances fully change. What is one trouble you are facing right now where we can join you in praising God for His deliverance ahead of time? Psalm 54:4-7
11
WEEK ONE
  1. Facing overwhelming pressure from a close companion, David expresses a desperate desire to fly away and escape the battle. When you feel the intense urge to run away from a difficult situation, how can we encourage you to cast your burden on the Lord instead? Psalm 55:6-8
  2. David concludes that while circumstances may crumble, he will confidently cast his cares on the Lord who sustains the righteous. What is a heavy burden you need to hand over to God today, and how can we support you in leaving it in His hands? Psalm 55:22-23

LIVING ON PURPOSE: FELLOWSHIP

Reach out to one person in the group this week with an encouraging text or phone call, reminding them of God's faithfulness in their specific struggle.

PRAYING TOGETHER

Let's pray that God will deepen our trust in His mercy and that we would become a community that authentically bears one another's burdens and points each other to the Rock.

"When I am afraid, I will put my trust in you."

DISCOVERY QUESTIONS

  2. The Psalmist finds comfort in knowing that God collects every tear and records our sorrows. When you feel misunderstood or overwhelmed, how does knowing God intimately cares for your pain change your perspective, and how can we remind each other of this? Psalm 56:8-9

PSALMS 51-75: A SMALL GROUP STUDY
  1. Hiding in a cave, David chooses to awaken his soul to praise rather than giving into despair. When you feel stuck in a 'cave' or a season of waiting, what practical steps can we take together to keep our hearts focused on God's purpose? Psalm 57:1-3
  2. David declares God's steadfast love and faithfulness even while surrounded by danger. How can we encourage one another to sing of God's greatness when our immediate circumstances look bleak? Psalm 57:7-10
  3. The Psalmist cries out against the injustice in the world, asking God to bring things right. When you see unfairness or injustice affecting your life or those around you, how can we support each other in trusting God as the ultimate judge rather than growing bitter? Psalm 58:1-11
  4. The Psalm confidently declares that God will ultimately reward the righteous. How can we keep each other anchored in the truth of God's eternal justice when the world feels upside down? Psalm 58:10-11
  5. While his house was being watched by enemies, David pictured God as his high fortress and strong tower. In what area of your life do you feel most vulnerable right now, and how can we help you envision God going before you as your protector? Psalm 59:9-10
  6. Instead of watching for the enemy's next move, David chooses to sing of God's strength in the morning. What is a negative situation where you need to shift your focus, and how can we hold you accountable to look for God's strength instead of the problem? Psalm 59:16-17
15
WEEK TWO
  1. Acknowledging a season of defeat, David realizes the need to run to God as a rallying banner rather than relying on his own strategy. Where have you been fighting battles in your own strength, and how can we encourage you to surrender that fight to God? Psalm 60:1-5
  2. The Psalm declares that human help is worthless compared to the victory God brings. How can we as a group ensure we are looking to God as our ultimate hope rather than just treating Him as a part of our life strategy? Psalm 60:11-12

LIVING ON PURPOSE: DISCIPLESHIP

Identify one specific area where you have been trying to win a battle in your own strength, and commit to spending ten minutes in prayer each morning this week asking God for His strategy and strength.

PRAYING TOGETHER

Let's pray for the courage to face our fears with active trust in God, asking Him to reveal where we need to surrender our own efforts and lean completely on His deliverance.

18
PSALMS 51-75: A SMALL GROUP STUDY
  1. David discovers that true soul rest comes from waiting in silence for God alone. In our loud and busy world, what is your biggest obstacle to experiencing quiet rest with God, and what practical changes can we encourage each other to make? Psalm 62:1-2
  2. We are invited to pour out our hearts to God at all times because He is our refuge. What heavy emotion or situation do you need to pour out to God today, and how can we stand with you in that vulnerability? Psalm 62:8
  3. Recognizing his deep spiritual thirst in a dry land, David earnestly seeks God's presence. In what ways do you sometimes try to satisfy your soul's thirst with things other than God, and how can we help each other pursue Him instead? Psalm 63:1-5
  4. David describes clinging closely to God through the watches of the night. When worries keep you awake, how can we encourage one another to shift our focus to God's upholding hand? Psalm 63:6-8
  5. Acknowledging the hidden snares and sharp words of the enemy, the Psalmist looks to God for ultimate protection. When you face unexpected criticism or spiritual attacks, how can we pray for your focus to remain on God's victory rather than the attack? Psalm 64:1-4
  6. The Psalm concludes that God will turn the enemy's plans upside down, leading the righteous to rejoice. How can we help each other remember the final victory of God when we are in the middle of a messy struggle? Psalm 64:7-10
19
WEEK THREE
  1. The Psalmist beautifully describes God's house as a place overflowing with forgiveness, hope, and abundance. Which of those characteristics—forgiveness, hope, or abundance—do you most need to experience this week, and how can we remind you of it? Psalm 65:3-5
  2. Creation itself is described as shouting for joy and singing at God's bountiful provision. How can we as a group cultivate a deeper sense of awe and joyful worship in our everyday routines? Psalm 65:9-13

LIVING ON PURPOSE: WORSHIP

Set an alarm for a specific time each day this week to pause for two minutes of complete silence, focusing solely on the character of God and thanking Him for His presence.

PRAYING TOGETHER

Let's pray that God would quiet our restless hearts, satisfy our deep thirst with His presence, and open our eyes to the wonder of His daily provision.

PSALMS 51-75: A SMALL GROUP STUDY
  1. The prayer for God's blessing is so that His ways may be known on earth and His salvation among all nations. When you think about the blessings in your life, how can we challenge each other to use them as a bridge to share God's love with those around us? Psalm 67:1-2
  2. The Psalmist calls for all the nations to be glad and sing for joy because God rules justly. Who is someone in your life that needs to hear about the joy of knowing God, and how can we pray for your interactions with them this week? Psalm 67:3-4
  3. Describing God as a father to the fatherless and a defender of widows, the Psalm highlights God's heart for the vulnerable. How can our group practically demonstrate this compassionate heart of God to someone in our community? Psalm 68:5-6
  4. The Psalmist praises God who daily bears our burdens and saves us. When you interact with people who are weighed down by life, how can we encourage you to point them to the One who desires to carry their load? Psalm 68:19
  5. Experiencing overwhelming waters of opposition, the Psalmist cries out for God's rescuing love. When you feel discouraged by how others react to your faith, how can we support you in keeping your focus on God's rescuing power? Psalm 69:14-16
  6. The Psalm looks forward to a time when the poor will see God's deliverance and be glad. In what ways can we share the hope of God's deliverance with those who are currently struggling in our spheres of influence? Psalm 69:32-33

WEEK FOUR

  2. The Psalmist declares that those who love God's salvation should always say, 'Let God be exalted!' What is one practical way you can verbally exalt God in your workplace or neighborhood this week, and how can we hold you accountable? Psalm 70:4

PSALMS 51-75: A SMALL GROUP STUDY
  1. Praying for the king, the Psalm envisions a leader who defends the afflicted and rescues the needy. How can we actively support and minister to the vulnerable or overlooked people in our own community? Psalm 72:12-14

  5. Remembering the devastation of the sanctuary, the Psalmist recalls the reality of God's past saving deeds (It Was You). When a ministry or project you care about seems to be falling apart, how can we remind each other of what God has already faithfully accomplished? Psalm 74:12-17
  6. The Psalm asks God to remember His covenant and not forget the lives of the afflicted. How can we pray for one another to keep serving faithfully even when we feel forgotten or unseen by others? Psalm 74:20-22

LIVING ON PURPOSE: MINISTRY

Look for a specific way to serve someone in your church or neighborhood who is older or perhaps feels overlooked, expressing value and care for them.

PRAYING TOGETHER
