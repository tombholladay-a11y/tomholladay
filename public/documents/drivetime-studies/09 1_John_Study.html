ABOUT DRIVETIME DEVOTIONS
SMALL GROUP STUDIES

Welcome to this special Drivetime Devotions, small group study. This five-week study is designed so that you can enjoy Drivetime Devotions as a podcast throughout the week and then talk about it with your small group. This will allow for more time in your small group for discussion, application, and fellowship.

The purpose of Drivetime Devotions is to help you know God's Word and put it into practice. It's usually better to study small sections of God's Word each day instead of a larger section once a week. Studying the Bible is like eating—you'll be healthier if you eat small amounts each day rather than one big meal a week! Episodes are designed to be listened to once per day, five days a week. In the event you miss a day or two, you can still stay on schedule.

A FEW TIPS BEFORE YOU DIG IN

LISTEN

Prepare yourself before each study by listening to each week's five daily podcast episodes (one per day).

CHECKING IN

Open each meeting with a time for everyone to check in on each other's progress with the podcast. Accountability is a key to success in this study!

KEY VERSE

Each week you will find a key verse (or verses) from the chapter you are studying. You may want to memorize this verse together as a group.

6
1 JOHN: A SMALL GROUP STUDY

DISCOVERY QUESTIONS

These are questions for group discussion. Please don't feel pressured to discuss every single question. The material in this study is meant to be your servant, not your master. Therefore there is no reason to rush through your discussions. Give everyone ample time to share their thoughts.

Some questions will reference the verses from which they are taken. Many groups will want to have someone read those verses accompanying the question. There is something powerful about hearing the Bible read aloud together as a group.

LIVING ON PURPOSE

This section helps you encourage each other in balancing God's five purposes of evangelism, discipleship, fellowship, ministry, and worship. Each week, there will be a prayer, practical action, or personal assignment that focuses on one of these purposes.

PRAYING TOGETHER

At the end of each session you will find suggestions for your group prayer time. Praying together is one of the greatest privileges of small group life. Please don't take it for granted.

Get ready for God to do incredible things in your life as you begin the adventure of learning more deeply about the most exciting message in the world: the truth about God!

WEEK ONE

CHECKING IN

  • If you are just starting together as a group, take time to go around the room and share your name, where you grew up, and what brought you to the group.
  • 1 John emphasizes that God is light and in Him is no darkness. What is one area of your life where you've seen God bring light recently?

KEY VERSE

"If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just and will forgive us our sins and purify us from all unrighteousness."
1 John 1:9 (NIV)

DISCOVERY QUESTIONS

  1. John wrote so that his readers would have fellowship and their joy would be complete. How are fellowship and joy connected in your experience? Verses 3-4
  2. What does it mean to "walk in the light" as opposed to "walking in the darkness"? Verses 5-7
  3. When you sin, are you more likely to hide, rationalize, or deny it? How does 1 John challenge those responses? Verses 8-10
  4. How does the promise that God is "faithful and just" to forgive us change the way you approach Him when you fail? Verse 9

WEEK TWO

CHECKING IN

  • How did it go this past week reaching out to encourage someone in the group?

KEY VERSE

"Whoever claims to live in him must walk as Jesus did."
1 John 2:6 (NIV)

DISCOVERY QUESTIONS

  1. What does it mean that Jesus is both our "advocate" and our "atoning sacrifice"? Verses 1-2
  2. How does John connect our love for God with our obedience to His commands? Verses 3-6
  3. Why is it impossible to walk in the light while hating a brother or sister in Christ? Verses 9-11
  4. How does the "world" (the cravings of sinful man, the lust of the eyes, and the boasting of what he has and does) pull us away from the love of the Father? Verses 15-17
  5. What encourages you most about the three stages of spiritual growth (children, young men, fathers) described in verses 12-14?

"How great is the love the Father has lavished on us, that we should be called children of God! And that is what we are!"
1 John 3:1 (NIV)

DISCOVERY QUESTIONS

  3. How do our actions reveal whether we are children of God or children of the devil? Verses 7-10
  4. How did Jesus demonstrate what true love looks like, and how are we called to imitate that? Verses 16-18
  5. What does it mean to "set our hearts at rest in his presence" when our hearts condemn us? Verses 19-20

KEY VERSE

"There is no fear in love. But perfect love drives out fear, because fear has to do with punishment."
1 John 4:18 (NIV)

DISCOVERY QUESTIONS

  1. Why is it important to "test the spirits," and what is the ultimate test of truth according to John? Verses 1-3
  2. How does knowing that "the one who is in you is greater than the one who is in the world" give you courage? Verse 4
  3. We are told that "God is love." What is the difference between saying "God is love" and the world's idea of love? Verses 7-10
  4. How does God's perfect love drive out fear and anxiety from our lives? Verses 17-18
  5. Why is it impossible to truly love God while holding onto hatred or bitterness toward another person? Verses 19-21

WEEK FIVE

CHECKING IN

  • How did reflecting on God's perfect love help you deal with any fears or anxieties this week?
  • What has been your biggest takeaway from our study of 1 John so far?

KEY VERSE

"And this is the testimony: God has given us eternal life, and this life is in his Son. He who has the Son has life; he who does not have the Son of God does not have life."
1 John 5:11-12 (NIV)

DISCOVERY QUESTIONS

  1. How is obedience to God's commands an evidence of our love for Him, and why are His commands not burdensome? Verses 1-3
  2. What does it mean to "overcome the world," and how does our faith make that possible? Verses 4-5
  3. God has given testimony about His Son. Why is it so crucial that we accept God's testimony over man's? Verses 9-12
  4. John writes so that we may "know" we have eternal life. How does this assurance give us confidence in our prayers? Verses 13-15
  5. Review the assurances in verses 18-20. Which of these promises brings you the most comfort today?
